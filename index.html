How an engagement works

The same four steps apply to every service.

  1. Intro call

    We talk through the problem. It’s free and takes about 30 minutes.

  2. Scope and quote

    You get a written plan, timeline, and price.

  3. Work in short cycles

    You see progress along the way, not only at the end.

  4. Handoff

    You get everything documented so your team can run it without me.

Common questions

What do you need from me to start?

Access to the data or a sample of it, a description of the decision the work supports, and the people who will use the result.

Which tools do you work in?

Power BI, Excel, SQL (PostgreSQL and MySQL), Python, SharePoint, and Power Automate. If you use something else, ask.

How do you handle sensitive data?

I work with the minimum data needed, and I can use masked or sample data when that’s an option.
